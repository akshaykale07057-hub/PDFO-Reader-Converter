# Privacy Policy for PDFO Reader & Converter

*Effective Date: October 20, 2026*

**PDFO Reader & Converter** ("we", "our", or "the App") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Summary (Privacy First)
- **Local Document Processing**: PDFO Reader & Converter operates primarily as an on-device utility. Your documents, PDFs, scanned pages, and converted files are processed **locally on your device**. We do not upload, read, or store your documents on any private servers.
- **No Personal Account Required**: You can use the core features of the App without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number.

---

## 2. Device Permissions We Request

To provide key features, the App requests access to specific device hardware and services:

1. **Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**:
   - **Purpose**: Allows you to scan paper documents to PDF and scan QR codes.
   - **Data Handling**: Camera frames are processed in real-time on your device. No photos or video recordings are transmitted to external servers without your explicit consent.

2. **Storage Permission (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**:
   - **Purpose**: Allows the App to discover, open, display, convert, and save PDF and Office files (DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, TXT) on your device.
   - **Data Handling**: File access is strictly local. We do not transmit or index your personal files remotely.

3. **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**:
   - **Purpose**: Required to serve advertisements via Google AdMob and check for app updates.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

### Google AdMob
We use **Google AdMob** to display banner and interstitial advertisements to keep the app free. Google AdMob may collect and process:
- IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
- Mobile Advertising ID (Google Play Advertising ID / GAID)
- App interaction and diagnostic crash reports
- For more details, review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google AdMob Data Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

---

## 4. Data Security
We prioritize data security by executing document conversion, viewing, and scanning operations locally on your hardware. We do not maintain any databases containing your documents or files.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
